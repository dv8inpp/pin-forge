03 · Retailers — affiliate link profiles

Amazon works out of the box. For any other retailer (Walmart, Best Buy, Target, eBay, or a merchant on ShareASale/CJ/Rakuten/Awin/Impact), add a profile here: a domain to match, and the deep-link template your network gave you, with {url} where the product page URL goes. No profile for a domain? You can still forge a pin — just paste the network's ready-made affiliate link for that product when prompted.
